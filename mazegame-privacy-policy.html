Maze Run: Tilt Maze Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Maze Run: Tilt Maze is a casual maze puzzle provided by an independent developer.

Information we do not collect directly

Maze Run does not require an account, login, camera, microphone, contacts, or access to user media. Maze state, elapsed time, and difficulty are used for app functionality and remain local or in memory unless a future version states otherwise.

Motion sensor use

The app uses the device accelerometer or gravity sensor to control the ball. Motion readings are processed on the device for gameplay and are not sent to the developer by the game code.

Advertising and analytics

Maze Run may use Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Firebase Analytics. These third-party SDKs may collect or process device identifiers, app activity, app diagnostics, approximate location derived from network signals, and advertising-related information for ads, analytics, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and app functionality.

Third-party services

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob privacy information
  • Firebase privacy and security

Children

Maze Run is not intentionally directed to children under 13. If the app is later distributed to children or through the Families program, this policy and the app's advertising and consent configuration will be updated as required.

Data deletion

The app does not provide account creation. Users can delete local app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. For developer-held support records, contact the support address below.
