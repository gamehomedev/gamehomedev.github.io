DropGrid: Block Puzzle Privacy Policy

Effective date:25.08.2026

DropGrid: Block Puzzle is a casual puzzle game provided by an independent developer.

Information we do not collect directly

DropGrid does not require an account, login, camera, microphone, contacts, or access to user media. Gameplay state such as score, level, and current board state is used for app functionality and is stored locally or in memory unless future versions say otherwise.

Advertising and analytics

DropGrid may use Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Firebase Analytics. These third-party SDKs may collect or process device identifiers, app activity, app diagnostics, approximate location derived from network signals, and advertising-related information for ads, analytics, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and app functionality.

Third-party services

  • AdMob privacy information
  • Firebase privacy and security

Children

DropGrid is not intentionally directed to children under 13. If you distribute the app to children or families, update this policy and configure ads/SDKs for the applicable requirements.

Data deletion

The app does not provide account creation. You can delete local app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. For developer-held support records, contact us using the support email below.
